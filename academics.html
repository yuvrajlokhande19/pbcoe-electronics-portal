Semester-by-Semester Flow

Click any semester to expand its core stack.


SEMESTER 1–2 · Foundations
Network Analysis · Engineering Mathematics · Basic Electronics Workshop · Programming in C · Engineering Graphics. Outcome: breadboard fluency + C fundamentals.
SEMESTER 3 · Digital Core
Digital System Design (HDL) · Analog Circuits-I · Signals & Systems · Electromagnetics-I · Data Structures. Lab: Verilog on Basys-3.
SEMESTER 4 · Communication Layer
Analog Communication · Analog Circuits-II · Microprocessors (8086→x86 concepts) · Control Systems · Probability & Random Processes.
SEMESTER 5 · Embedded Turn
Microcontrollers & Embedded C · Digital Communication · Antenna Theory · DSP Fundamentals · Elective-I (IoT / Robotics). Mini-project: bare-metal firmware.
SEMESTER 6 · Advanced Stack
VLSI Design · Microwave Engineering · CMOS Analog IC Foundations · Wireless Networks · Elective-II (Embedded Security / SDR).
SEMESTER 7 · Specialization
Physical Design Automation · Test & Verification · Industry Internship (8 weeks min.) · Major Project Phase-I.
SEMESTER 8 · Launch
Major Project Phase-II (publishable/tape-out target) · Semiconductor Business & IP seminar · Comprehensive viva.
