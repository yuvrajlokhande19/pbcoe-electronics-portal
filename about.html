The Department, Decoded.

Three decades of turning curious students into circuit designers, chip engineers and systems architects.

EST. 1994//NBA ACCREDITED ×3 CYCLES//NAAC 'A' GRADE INSTITUTE//AICTE APPROVED//UNIVERSITY GOLD MEDALISTS: 11//
◎

Mission

To produce electronics engineers who don't just pass exams but can walk into any semiconductor lab, embedded team or RF bench and be useful from day one — through relentless hands-on practice, industry-grade tooling and mentorship that treats every student as a future colleague.

▲

Vision

To be the reference department for undergraduate electronics education in the region — the place where India's next generation of VLSI, embedded and communications talent is discovered, trained and launched into the world's most critical industry.

// FROM THE HOD'S DESK

Dr. Anjali Mehra · Head of Department


"When I joined this department in 2003, we had one oscilloscope shared between forty students. Today a fresher here tape-outs their first FPGA design within two semesters. That trajectory is exactly what we protect: every rupee, every hour goes into making students industry-dangerous. The portal you're browsing is part of that promise — our curriculum is public, our labs are open, and our results speak at 94% placement."

Signal History

Milestones on our way from a single lab to a full-stack electronics education hub.



1994

Department Founded

ECE branch opens with 60 seats, one electronics lab and a faculty of five.

2001

First NBA Accreditation

Program accredited; VLSI elective introduced ahead of most regional colleges.

2009

Embedded Systems Lab

Texas Instruments partners to set up an ARM-based embedded teaching facility.

2015

FPGA Suite + IEEE Branch

Xilinx donation lands 40 Basys boards; IEEE Student Branch chartered.

2021

RF & Antenna Range

VNA-backed RF lab opens; department begins defense-sector consultancy work.

2024

Electronics Portal v1.0

Digital transformation: simulators, arcade learning and career intelligence go online.

2026

Portal v2.0 — You Are Here

Full SaaS-style experience with live simulation, games and opportunity radar.
