// FAQ

Frequently Asked Signals


Is hostel accommodation available?
Yes — separate boys' and girls' hostels with fiber internet, study halls and a shared electronics workbench room open till midnight.
I'm from a non-PCM background. Can I switch in?
Direct B.Tech entry requires PCM at 10+2. Consider our Diploma program route, which accepts any 10th pass student and feeds into second-year B.Tech via lateral entry.
What makes ECE at PBCOE different?
Hardware-first culture: every theory course has a mandatory bench component, first-year students receive a personal maker kit, and lab access extends far beyond timetable hours.
Are internships guaranteed?
The semester-7 internship is facilitated by the department's industry MoUs and the portal's Opportunity Engine — placement is not literally guaranteed but the pipeline has placed 100% of eligible students for three consecutive years.
Fee structure and payment plans?
Tuition is university-regulated; semester-wise payment at no extra cost, education-loan desk on campus, and the scholarship matrix above can reduce tuition up to 100%.
