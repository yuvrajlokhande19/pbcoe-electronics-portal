Core Academic & Hands-on Laboratory Courses

Approved RTMNU NEP Scheme for B.Tech Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

ET501T

Embedded Systems

Faculty: Mr. Suhas D. Kakde (Academic Coordinator)

Microcontroller architecture, ARM Cortex programming, timers, UART/SPI/I2C communication, and sensor interfacing.

4 Credits ET503P Lab Co-requisite
ET502T

Communication Electronics

Faculty: Mrs. Jayshri Salodkar (Shelke)

Modulation schemes, RF transceivers, superheterodyne receivers, noise analysis, and digital link budgets.

4 Credits ET504P Lab Co-requisite
ET505T

Signals & Systems

Faculty: Mrs. Nisha N. Naukarkar

Continuous and discrete-time signals, LTI systems, convolution, Fourier transform, and Z-transform system stability.

3 Credits RTMNU Theory
ET506-1T

Digital System Design using VHDL

Faculty: Dr. Jayant Y. Hande

VHDL syntax, behavioral modeling, finite state machines (FSM), testbenches, and FPGA hardware synthesis.

3 Credits ET507P FPGA Lab
IOT-NODE

Incharge: Mr. Suhas D. Kakde & Dr. Jayant Hande

Real-time R307 optical fingerprint scanner connected to ESP32 node and Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL backend.
