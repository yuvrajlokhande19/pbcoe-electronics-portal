Why GPIO first?

Every embedded engineer's "hello world" is a blinking LED. Here you drive a whole byte — and read it back in binary, hex and decimal like real firmware.

L2

Why waveforms?

Scope literacy separates operators from observers. Recognizing square vs sawtooth vs PWM at a glance is a semester-3 superpower.

Why VHDL syntax?

Muscle memory for entity–architecture structure means lab exams feel like typing practice, not archaeology.
