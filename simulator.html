What am I looking at?

A series RLC driven by an ideal 5 V RMS source. The yellow dashed path animates conventional current — its speed tracks magnitude.

Why does Z dip?

X_L grows with frequency while X_C shrinks. At f₀ they cancel exactly and only R limits current — the definition of resonance.
