3. Introduction & Theoretical Principles of Biometric Sensing

Biometric authentication utilizes unique physiological human traits (fingerprint ridge patterns) for automated person identification. Fingerprints consist of elevated friction ridges and valleys. While ridge patterns follow general archetypes (loops, whorls, and arches), individuality is determined by local ridge anomalies termed Minutiae Points — predominantly ridge endings (abrupt terminations) and ridge bifurcations (single ridges dividing into two branches).

Optical Sensing Physics (Frustrated Total Internal Reflection - FTIR):
The R307 sensor employs an optical glass prism illuminated by internal blue/red LEDs at an angle exceeding the critical angle ($\theta_c$). When dry air contacts the glass surface, Total Internal Reflection (TIR) occurs, directing all light onto the internal CMOS imaging array (bright field). When a student places a finger against the glass, the skin ridges make direct optical contact, disrupting TIR and absorbing light (dark ridges). Valleys retain air gaps and reflect light. The CMOS sensor digitizes this $256 \times 288$ pixel image at 500 DPI resolution.

The internal DSP in the R307 converts raw optical bitmaps into a compact 512-byte mathematical characterization file (CharBuffer). During enrollment, two separate optical scans are captured and mathematically fused into a master template stored in non-volatile flash memory across flash slots #1 to #127.

6. System Architecture & End-to-End Telemetry Pipeline

The project establishes an automated multi-layer IoT ecosystem. Biometric vectors captured at the physical edge are validated, authenticated against local flash memory, and dispatched instantaneously across the internet to the cloud PostgreSQL database.

Fig 4.1: Technical Engineering Architecture — Edge Sensing, ESP32 Cloud Gateway & Supabase PostgreSQL Stream
1. Edge Verification Engine

The R307 compares live optical scans against its internal 512-byte templates in flash. Upon match, the slot ID (1 to 65) and confidence score are returned over UART in <280ms.

2. Supabase Cloud Pipeline

ESP32 constructs a JSON telemetry payload and executes an HTTP POST request over TLS 1.3 to /rest/v1/attendance_logs, triggering immediate client updates.

7. Dual-Pass Optical Enrollment State Machine

To prevent erroneous partial scans, candidate registration strictly enforces a 4-state dual-pass protocol executed cooperatively between the web portal and the embedded firmware:

State 1: Primary Optical Acquisition: Sensor LED activates. Candidate places finger. Image 1 converted to character buffer 1 (CharBuffer1). Buzzer chimes single 80ms pulse.
State 2: Finger Removal Polling: Loop polls finger.getImage() until FINGERPRINT_NOFINGER is asserted, preventing identical image double-capture.
State 3: Secondary Validation Pass: Candidate touches sensor again. Image 2 converted to CharBuffer2.
State 4: Vector Fusion & Flash Commitment: Firmware invokes finger.createModel(). If correlation coefficient > 92%, combined template is committed to targeted slot ID (1–65). Double chime indicates success.
